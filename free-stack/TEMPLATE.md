# Rules for every guide

1. **No step is "configure it."** Every step is a command to type or a button to click.
2. **The concept comes after the win.** Show it working, then explain why. Never open with theory.
3. **Say what is not free in the first 30 seconds.** Credibility comes from the disclosure.
4. **Every code block is complete.** No `// ...rest of your code here`. They will paste exactly what you give them.
5. **Every claim links to the vendor's own page.** You are asking people to trust a stranger's cost math. Let them check it.
6. **Troubleshooting has at least five real entries.** Write them from your own failures during the build.
7. **Voice: short declarative sentences. No dashes. Receipts, not adjectives.**
8. **Never say "simply," "just," or "easy."** If it were easy they would have done it already.
9. **Assume zero prior knowledge and zero patience.** They are on a phone in a truck.
10. **One guide, one outcome.** If it needs two outcomes, it is two guides.
