# Code of conduct

Be respectful, be direct, assume good faith. This project is maintained by one
person in their spare time, for other small-business owners to actually use —
that context should shape how you raise problems.

- Disagree with a decision? Say so, with reasons, in the issue or PR. Personal
  attacks, harassment, or repeatedly re-litigating a closed decision aren't.
- Reporting a bug or security issue is welcome and appreciated, even a blunt
  one. "This is broken because X" is always fine. "You're incompetent for
  writing this" is not.
- Off-topic, spam, or promotional content in issues/PRs will be closed without
  much discussion.

Violations can be reported to the maintainer directly (see contact info on the
GitHub repository). Repeated or serious violations may result in being blocked
from the repository.
