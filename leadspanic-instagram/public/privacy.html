[Your Business Name] is a private tool used by the operator of the Instagram account(s) @[your_instagram_handle] to schedule posts and to respond to comments and direct messages on that account. It is not offered to the public, has no user accounts, and no one signs up for it.

What is collected

When you comment on, or send a direct message to, one of these Instagram accounts, this tool receives and stores the following from Meta:

  • Your Instagram app-scoped user ID. This is an identifier Meta generates specifically for this application. It is not your Instagram account ID and cannot be used to identify you elsewhere.
  • Your Instagram username, where Meta provides it.
  • The text of the comment or message you sent, and the text of any reply sent back to you.
  • The time of each interaction.
  • If you click a link sent to you by this tool, the fact that the link was clicked, the time, and the referring page and browser user-agent string sent by your browser.
No email addresses, phone numbers, payment details, precise location, or contact lists are collected. Nothing is collected from anyone who does not first contact one of these accounts.

Why it is collected

  • To send you the link or information you asked for by commenting a keyword.
  • To answer your direct message.
  • To let the account operator read the conversation and reply personally.
  • To count how many people used each keyword, in aggregate.

How it is stored

Data is stored in Cloudflare D1 and Cloudflare R2, in the operator's own Cloudflare account. Access credentials are encrypted at rest. The application is reachable only by the operator, behind a bearer token. No data is sold, rented, or shared with advertisers, data brokers, or any third party. There is no third-party analytics or tracking software in this tool.

Message content is sent to Anthropic's Claude API for the sole purpose of drafting a reply to you. Anthropic processes it as a service provider and does not use it to train models. See Anthropic's privacy policy.

How long it is kept

  • Contacts, conversations, and messages: kept while the account operates, so a conversation can be picked up where it left off.
  • Activity and event logs: automatically deleted after 90 days.
  • Link-click records: kept for aggregate counting.
  • Backups: dated snapshots stored in the operator's own private storage.

Deleting your data

You can have everything associated with you deleted, in either of these ways:

  • Through Instagram. In your Instagram settings, remove this application's access. Meta then sends an automated deletion request to this tool, which deletes your contact record and every conversation, message, and click record attached to it, and returns a confirmation code you can check.
  • By asking. Send a direct message to the account asking for your data to be deleted, and it will be removed.

Deletion is immediate and permanent. Deleted records are not recoverable, though they may persist in a dated backup snapshot until that snapshot ages out.

Children

This tool is not directed at children and no data is knowingly collected from anyone under 13.
